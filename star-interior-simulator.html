Binary Parameters

5.00 M☉

The initially more massive star. Massive stars evolve faster, so this one swells into a giant first — the classic setup for the "Algol paradox."

What the primary is dumping mass onto shapes the whole outcome — a companion star, or a compact object already left behind by an earlier, faster-evolving star.
